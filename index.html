12 Project Rebound housing initiative
Housing stability · Academic momentum

Housing should not stand between a scholar and a degree.

Project Rebound’s Housing Initiative helps eligible Rebound Scholars address housing barriers through individualized planning, short-term assistance, and connections to longer-term stability.

01
Start with a conversation

Meet privately with Project Rebound staff to discuss your circumstances, other available resources, and the most appropriate next step.

Experiencing housing instability?Visit SMSU South 116 or call (909) 537-4351.Assistance depends on eligibility, available funding, documentation, and program approval.
Immediate

Last Gap

One-time support for an urgent housing shortfall after other available resources have been explored.

Transitional

Decreasing Stipend

Time-limited assistance that gradually decreases while the scholar works toward sustainable housing.

Campus-based

Dorm Assistance

Financial aid or a student contribution is applied first; approved Project Rebound assistance may help cover the remaining gap and decreases over time.

Long-term vision

Coyote House

A restorative, education-centered housing community designed to support belonging, stability, and degree completion.

Growth. Resilience. Achievement. Building futures together.
